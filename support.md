# BeadLight Support

BeadLight turns your iPad into a light table for fuse beads (Perler, Hama,
Artkal). Place a transparent pegboard on the screen, tap a color, and place
beads exactly where they glow.

## Quick start

1. Open BeadLight and follow the interactive guide (reopen it anytime with
   the **?** button on the home screen).
2. Import any pattern image with **Import Pattern**, or open a built-in
   sample.
3. Lay a clear pegboard on the screen, use **Align** to line the grid up
   under it, then tap **Lock** so stray touches can't move anything.
4. Tap a color in the Colors panel — only that color's beads light up.
   Place them, mark the color done, and move to the next one.

## Common questions

**My pattern imported at the wrong size.**
Turn off "Auto-detect grid" in the import screen and set the board size
manually with the slider.

**The screen doesn't respond.**
The board is probably locked — tap the **Unlock** button in the bottom-left
corner.

**How does the free trial work?**
Everything is free for 7 days after your first launch. After that, keep
building with a $4.99/month subscription or a one-time $49.99 purchase.
Deleting and reinstalling the app does not restart the trial.

**How do I restore a purchase on a new iPad?**
Open the paywall (tap the Premium/Trial pill on the home screen) and tap
**Restore Purchases** while signed into the same Apple Account.
