# BeadLight

**Your iPad is a light table for fuse beads.**

Lay a transparent pegboard directly on the screen. Tap a color, and only
that color's bead positions light up beneath your board — place them, mark
the color done, and move on. No more counting squares on a printed chart.

- **Guided assembly** — beads glow exactly where they go, color by color
- **Import any pattern** — pixel art, chart screenshots, or photos, with
  automatic grid detection for a pixel-perfect import
- **Real bead palettes** — Perler, Hama, and Artkal, with optional dithering
- **Made for real boards** — align the grid under your pegboard, then lock
  the screen so nothing moves while you work

Free for 7 days, then $4.99/month or a one-time $49.99 purchase.
No accounts, no ads, no tracking — your projects stay on your iPad.
