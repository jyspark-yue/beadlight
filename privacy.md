# BeadLight Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 15, 2026_

BeadLight is an iPad app that turns your screen into a light table for fuse
bead crafting. Your privacy is simple with us, because we collect nothing.

## Data collection

**BeadLight does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- There are no accounts and no sign-in.
- Your projects and imported images are stored only on your device, inside
  the app's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
  tracking of any kind.
- The app makes no network requests except those made by Apple's StoreKit
  framework to process purchases through your Apple Account.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase are processed entirely by
Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The date of
your first launch is stored on your device (in the system Keychain) solely
to run the 7-day free trial.

## Photos

If you import a pattern from your photo library, the image is processed
on-device and a bead-grid copy is saved with your project. The app uses the
system photo picker, which means it can only access the specific photos you
choose.

## Children

BeadLight is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children.
